Color and bleach prices
by hair length

The color menu is divided into Special, Premium, Prestige, Bleach A and B, and Design Color (partial or full), with prices by S, M and L length.

For S length there are also men's color (cut included) and men's special color. We look at your face shape and hair type in a one-on-one consultation.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants to check prices by length beforehand
  • Anyone wanting bleach or design color
  • Men looking for color with a cut included
  • Anyone who wants to choose a color after a consultation

FAQ

FAQ

How are color prices divided?
By S, M and L length and by menu. For example, the regular price for Special is ₩130,000 (S), ₩150,000 (M) and ₩170,000 (L).
How much is bleaching?
Bleach A regular price is ₩110,000 (S), ₩130,000 (M), ₩150,000 (L). Bleach B regular price is ₩130,000 (S), ₩150,000 (M), ₩170,000 (L).
Do you have men's color options?
Yes. The S length menu includes men's color with cut at a regular price of ₩100,000 and men's special color at ₩150,000.
