Home/Perms

From women's to men's perms,
a specialist for each

Designers each focus on their own area of women's perms (Elizabeth, layered, hush, jelly, hippie) and men's perms (shadow, see-through, iron, down).

In a one-on-one consultation we look at your face shape and hair type and suggest a perm that is easy to manage at home and lasts. We also consult on root perms and on repair perms for heat-damaged and damaged hair.

Recommended for

  • Anyone wanting a perm that is easy to manage at home
  • Anyone wanting a women's perm such as layered, hush or Elizabeth
  • Anyone wanting a men's perm such as shadow, see-through, iron or down
  • Anyone with heat-damaged or damaged hair who wants a repair perm consultation
