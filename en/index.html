About

A salon that looks at your face shape
and hair type first.

RIAHN Hair Anyang 1st Street (리안헤어 안양일번가점) is on the 2nd floor of 36, Jangnae-ro 139beon-gil, in the Anyang 1st Street shopping area near Anyang Station. Opened in 2015, the salon has 15 styling seats and six designers including the director, offering women's perms, men's perms, bob and chin-length cuts, root perms, repair perms, color and bleaching.

In a one-on-one consultation we look at your face shape and hair type, then suggest cuts and perms that are easy to manage at home and last. We are open every day: weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. There is no dedicated parking, so public transit is recommended.

What sets us apart

  • Six designers, including the director, each focus on their own area of women's perms (Elizabeth, layered, hush, jelly, hippie) and men's perms (shadow, see-through, iron, down).
  • Repair perms for heat-damaged and damaged hair, plus a three-level clinic: Special, Premium and Prestige.
  • Consultations for mature women's styles such as bob cuts, chin-length cuts and root perms.
  • 15 styling seats, open every day of the year.
  • Member price on cuts (10% off) when you sign up as a member.
