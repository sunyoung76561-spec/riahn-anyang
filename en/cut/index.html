FAQ

FAQ

How does the member price work?
Members get the member price on cuts (10% off). For example, a men's cut is ₩22,000, or ₩19,800 for members; a women's cut is ₩24,000, or ₩21,600 for members.
Who should I book for a men's cut?
Men's cuts are a specialty of designer Saebyeok and senior designer Yeoul. You can book through Naver or by phone.
Who should I book for a bob or chin-length cut?
Bob and chin-length cuts are a specialty of director Jesi, who also offers root perms and neat styles fitted to your face shape.
