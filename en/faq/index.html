Home/FAQ
How do I book?
Through Naver Booking or by phone (031-381-0830).
Is there parking?
There is no dedicated parking. Please use public parking near Anyang Station. We recommend coming via Anyang Station (Seoul Subway Line 1).
Do you provide parking tickets?
No, parking tickets are not provided.
What are your hours and days off?
We are open every day: weekdays 11:00–20:00, Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00. Please check each designer's days off on Naver Booking.
What are your prices?
Men's cut ₩22,000 and women's cut ₩24,000. Men's basic perm ₩100,000 and women's basic perm ₩110,000 (regular). S length Special color ₩130,000 (regular). Special clinic ₩70,000 (regular). See each service page for full prices.
Is there a member discount?
Members get the member price on cuts (10% off).
This is my first visit. How do I choose a designer?
Layered cuts and perms: Yeoul, Gaeul, Jea. Hush cuts and perms: Gaeul. Elizabeth and repair perms: Jea. Jelly and hippie perms: Momo. Bob, chin-length cuts and root perms: Jesi. Iron perms: Saebyeok.
Do you specialize in men's perms?
Yes. Designers each focus on men's perms such as shadow, see-through, iron and down perms.
Can I get advice for heat-damaged or damaged hair?
Yes. We offer repair perms and a three-level clinic (Special, Premium, Prestige).
Do you consult on mature women's styles?
Yes, including bob cuts, chin-length cuts and root perms.

For anything else, please contact us by phone.
