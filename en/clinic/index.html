Three levels of clinic
to suit your hair

We offer a three-level clinic: Special, Premium and Prestige. Prices are the same for S, M and L length.

If your hair is heat-damaged or damaged, ask about repair perms as well. Some designer event packages include a clinic.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants to care for hair after a perm or color
  • Anyone with heat-damaged or damaged hair
  • Anyone looking for a clinic priced the same for every length

FAQ

FAQ

Do clinic prices change with hair length?
No. Prices are the same for S, M and L length. Regular prices are ₩70,000 for Special, ₩100,000 for Premium and ₩150,000 for Prestige.
How many clinic levels are there?
Three: Special, Premium and Prestige.
Are there packages with a perm?
Yes. Jea Signature (Elizabeth perm + clinic, ₩169,000, when you post a Naver photo review) and Momo Signature (jelly or hippie perm, premium product + premium clinic, ₩149,000). These are Naver Booking event prices and may change depending on the period and conditions.
